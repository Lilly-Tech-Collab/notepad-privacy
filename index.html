Privacy Policy

Notepad: Simple Notes (Android) · Lilly Tech Systems, LLC · Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Notepad: Simple Notes app (the "App"), published by Lilly Tech Systems, LLC ("we", "us"), handles information. We built the App so that your notes stay private.

1. Your notes stay on your device

Everything you write in the App (note titles, text, checklists, colors, pins, and items in Trash) is stored only on your device. We do not upload, read, sell, or share your notes, and we have no servers that receive them. If you have turned on Android's own device backup (for example Google One backup), Android may include the App's notes in that backup. Google handles that backup under your Google account settings, not us.

When you choose Share or Save as .txt file, the note is passed to the app or location you pick. That is under your control.

2. Advertising

The App is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location derived from IP addressAdvertising, fraud prevention
App interactions with ads, diagnostics and performance dataAnalytics, fraud prevention, ad delivery

This data is sent encrypted to Google. It is not linked to your notes. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US states): Where the law requires it, the App asks for your consent with Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Delete your data: Deleting notes, emptying Trash, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App permanently removes your notes from the device.

4. Permissions

The App uses only the Internet and network-state permissions, which are needed to load ads. It does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, files, or precise location.

5. Children

The App is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help address it.

6. Security

Notes are stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read under Android's security model. Ad-related data is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete, or opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell your notes. For ad-related data processed by Google, use the in-app Ad privacy choices and your device's ad settings, or contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. If we do, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
